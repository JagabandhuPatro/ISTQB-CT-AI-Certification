## 📝 Steps to Write the ISTQB CT-AI Exam

If you are planning to take the exam, follow these steps:

### 1. Verify Prerequisites
Before you can sit for the CT-AI exam, you must hold the **ISTQB Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL)** certification. Ensure you have your certificate number handy, as providers will verify this.

### 2. Download the Official Syllabus
Always anchor your studies to the official syllabus. Download the latest CT-AI syllabus from the https://istqb.org/certifications/certified-tester-ai-testing-ct-ai/ . Pay close attention to the K-levels (K1 to K4) for each learning objective to understand how deeply you need to know each topic.

### 3. Book Your Exam via an Exam Provider
Register for the exam through an official ISTQB exam provider such as **iSQI** or **GASQ**. 
* Select the "ISTQB Certified Tester AI Testing" exam.
* Choose your preferred language.
* Select the exam format: **Remote Proctoring** (take it from home) or **Test Center** (e.g., Pearson VUE).

### 4. Prepare for Exam Day (If taking online)
If you opt for remote proctoring:
* Perform the system test provided by the exam board a few days beforehand.
* Ensure you have a quiet, clean, and well-lit room.
* Have a valid government-issued ID ready for the proctor to verify your identity.

### 5. Take the Exam
The exam typically consists of 40 multiple-choice questions, and you have 60 minutes to complete it (non-native speakers may get an additional 15 minutes). A passing score is 65% (at least 26 correct answers).

### 6. Claim Your Digital Badge
Once you pass, your exam provider will issue your results and a digital badge.
